# Pinbook Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Pinbook is designed as a private, offline-first expense notebook. The current version does not require an account and does not send personal data, financial records, receipts, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or diagnostics to Pinbook or its developer.

## Data stored on your device

Books, expenses, payments, templates, settings, and receipt metadata are stored locally on your device. Receipt images you explicitly select are copied into Pinbook's private app storage. Pinbook uses this information only to provide features you request inside the app.

## Backups, exports, and sharing

Pinbook can create local backups, PDF statements, and CSV statements. A file leaves Pinbook only when you explicitly choose a destination through Apple's Files or Share interface. If you choose iCloud Drive or another third-party file provider, that provider processes the file under its own privacy policy. Pinbook does not operate a cloud service and does not receive these files.

## Notifications

If you enable a reminder, Pinbook schedules a local notification on your device. Reminder text is deliberately generic. Notification permission is requested only when you choose a reminder feature.

## Tracking, analytics, and advertising

Pinbook does not track you, does not show advertising, does not use third-party analytics, and does not sell or share personal data.

## Data retention and deletion

Your app data remains on your device until you remove it in Pinbook or delete the app. Deleting Pinbook removes its local app container, subject to Apple's device and backup behavior. Copies you exported to Files remain under your control and must be deleted from their saved locations separately.

## Children

Pinbook is a general-purpose financial organization tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before a future version introduces optional cloud synchronization, analytics, accounts, or any other material change to data handling.

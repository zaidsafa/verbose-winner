# Pinbook Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Pinbook is designed as a private, offline-first expense notebook. It does not require a Pinbook account and does not send personal data, financial records, receipts, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or diagnostics to Pinbook or its developer. Your records stay on your device by default. If you explicitly enable Google Drive sync, Pinbook sends the backup content described below only to the private app-data folder in your own Google account.

## Data stored on your device

Books, expenses, payments, templates, settings, and receipt metadata are stored locally on your device. Receipt images you explicitly select are copied into Pinbook's private app storage. Pinbook uses this information only to provide features you request inside the app.

## Backups, exports, and sharing

Pinbook can create local backups, PDF statements, and CSV statements. A file leaves Pinbook only when you explicitly choose a destination through Apple's Files or Share interface. If you choose iCloud Drive or another third-party file provider, that provider processes the file under its own privacy policy. Pinbook does not operate a cloud service and does not receive these files.

## Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Pinbook requests only Google's private app-data-folder permission. This permission lets Pinbook store and retrieve its own backup files; it does not let Pinbook view your other Google Drive files. Sync copies your Pinbook records, settings, and any receipt content included in the backup to that private folder so your devices can merge the same records. Google processes and stores those files under Google's privacy policy. Pinbook and its developer do not receive or access them.

Google Drive access tokens remain in device memory. The refresh credential needed to keep your chosen connection active is stored in Apple's device-only Keychain and is not included in Pinbook backups. You can use Sync now at any time or enable automatic sync when Pinbook opens.

## Notifications

If you enable a reminder, Pinbook schedules a local notification on your device. Reminder text is deliberately generic. Notification permission is requested only when you choose a reminder feature.

## Tracking, analytics, and advertising

Pinbook does not track you, does not show advertising, does not use third-party analytics, and does not sell or share personal data.

## Data retention and deletion

Your local app data remains on your device until you remove it in Pinbook or delete the app. Deleting Pinbook removes its local app container, subject to Apple's device and backup behavior. Copies exported to Files remain under your control and must be deleted from their saved locations separately.

Disconnecting Google Drive stops future sync and removes Pinbook's Google credential from the device, but it does not delete backup files already stored in Google's private app-data folder. You can remove those stored app data files through your Google account controls.

## Children

Pinbook is a general-purpose financial organization tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before a future version introduces analytics, advertising, a Pinbook account service, or any other material change to data handling.
